Core Rules Reference

Setup

  1. Assign Factions & Starting Player: Assign one faction to each player in any way.
  2. Place Score Markers: Each player places their score marker on “0” on the score track.
  3. Draw Starting Hands: Each Player draws 3 cards.
  4. Place Ruins: Place a ruin in each slot on the map marked with “R”.
  5. Form Item Supply: Place these items on the corresponding spaces of the item supply near the top of the map (2 boots, 2 satchels, 1 crossbow, 1 hammer, 2 swords, 2 kettles, and 2 stacks of coins).
  6. Gather Other Pieces: Hand out the 16 faction overview cards as desired, and place the two dice near the map.
  7. Set Up Factions: In setup order (A, B, C, etc.), each player follows their faction’s setup instructions.

Key Concepts

Cards

  • Birds are Wild Bird - Treated as a card of another suit.
  • Ambush Cards - 5 Ambush cards, can’t be crafted, may play one in battle to deal immediate hits.
  • Dominance Cards - 4 Dominance cards, can’t be crafted, may play one to change your victory condition.

Rule

  • A player rules a clearing if they have more total warriors and buildings in it than each other player.

Victory

How To Win

  • First to reach 30 VPs immediately wins the game.
  • When multiple players achieve 30 VPs simultaneously, the closest in clockwise order to the current player wins.

Scoring Victory Points

  • Removing Buildings & Tokens - You score a VP for each one removed.
  • Crafting Items - When you craft an item, score the VPs listed on the card.

Dominance Cards

  • Activating:
    • During your Daylight, if you have at least 10 VPs, you may activate a dominance card.
    • Remove your score marker, as you no longer score VPs.
  • Mouse, Rabbit, or Fox Dominance - Win immediately if you rule 3 clearings of the matching suit at the start of your Birdsong.
  • Bird Dominance - Win immediately if you rule 2 clearings in opposite corners at the start of your Birdsong.

Key Actions

Craft

  • Cost - To craft a card, you must activate crafting pieces of the suits listed in the card’s bottom-left corner.
  • Immediate Effects:
    • Paper box means resolve its effect and discard the card.
    • If it shows an item, take it from the item supply on the map. If not in supply, it can’t be crafted.
  • Persistent Effects - Stone box is placed in your play area, providing a continuous effect.
  • No Duplicates - Can’t craft an identical persistent effect to one already in your play area.

Move

  • Take any number of warriors/pawns from one clearing and move them along a link to an adjacent clearing.
  • Requirement: You must rule the origin clearing, destination clearing, or both.
  • A piece can be moved any number of times per turn.

Battle

  1. Defender May Ambush: Plays matching ambush card to deal 2 immediate hits. Attacker can match it to cancel.
  2. Use Before-Roll Effects: Resolve effects that trigger before rolling.
  3. Roll Dice: Attacker takes higher roll, defender takes lower roll. Max rolled hits = number of your warriors in battle.
  4. Use After-Roll Effects: Resolve modifications to battle calculation.
  5. Count Hits: Add extra hits (e.g., +1 if defender is defenseless).
  6. Deal Hits: Remove pieces. Warriors must be removed before buildings or tokens.

🐱 Marquise de Cat (The Industrial Invaders)

Monopolize the Woodland economy through mass production, logistics, and military garrisons.

Faction Rules & Abilities

  • The Keep: Only you can place pieces in the clearing with your Keep token. Enemy pieces can never be placed there. If destroyed, it is gone forever!
  • Field Hospitals: Whenever your warriors are removed from a clearing, spend a card matching that clearing's suit to immediately respawn them safely back at the Keep instead of the supply pool.
  • Crafting Engine: You craft cards during Daylight by activating your built Workshops matching the required clearing suits.

Faction Setup

  1. Form Pools: Gather 25 warriors and 8 wood tokens into your faction supply.
  2. Place the Keep: Place your Keep token in any one of the 4 corner clearings. This serves as your primary stronghold.
  3. Garrison the Woodland: Place exactly 1 warrior in every single clearing on the board except the clearing diagonally opposite your Keep.
  4. Deploy Infrastructure: Place 1 Sawmill, 1 Workshop, and 1 Recruiter among your Keep clearing and any adjacent clearings in any combination.
  5. Fill the Board Tracks: Place your remaining 5 Sawmills, 5 Workshops, and 5 Recruiters onto their respective faction board tracks from right to left.

Strategic Strengths

  • Starts the game in absolute control of almost the entire map.
  • High early-game score potential due to incredibly cheap initial buildings.
  • Perfect candidates for Dominance Cards because they already start ruling the clearing counts.

Critical Weaknesses

  • Natural target for all other players from turn one due to their board presence.
  • VP generation falls off drastically late-game as building costs escalate.
  • If the Keep token is destroyed, recovering is near impossible.
  • Losing Sawmills paralyzes the entire wood supply line economy.

🦅 Eyrie Dynasties (The Fractured Nobility)

Reclaim the ancient forest canopy by programming an escalating chain of political commands.

Faction Rules & Abilities

  • Lords of the Forest: You rule a clearing whenever you are tied for total presence (warriors + buildings), provided you have at least 1 piece there.
  • Disdain for Trade: Whenever you craft an item card, ignore the printed victory points and score exactly 1 VP instead (unless led by the Builder).
  • Crafting: Craft before resolving the Decree during Daylight by activating roosts.

Faction Setup

  1. Gather Warriors: Collect your 20 warriors.
  2. Place Roost & Starting Warriors: Place 1 Roost and 6 warriors in a corner clearing (diagonally opposite the Cat Keep if possible).
  3. Appoint Leader: Select an active Leader card. Take your 2 Loyal Vizier cards (Wildcard Birds) and tuck them into the starting columns specified by that leader.
  4. Tuck Viziers: Tuck your 2 Loyal Vizier cards, showing their suit, into the Decree columns above your faction board as listed on your leader.
  5. Fill Roosts Track: Place your 6 remaining roosts on your roost track from right to left.

Turn Phases & Action Economy

Birdsong

Emergency Orders: If you have no cards in your hand, draw one card.

Add to the Decree: Add one or two cards to the decree, revealing them from your hand and tucking them (Only one card can be bird each turn).

A New Roost: If you have no roosts on the map, place a roost and 3 warriors in a clearing with the fewest warriors where all those pieces can be placed.

Turmoil

  2. Purge: Discard all of the cards on the Decree except your Loyal Viziers.
  3. Depose: Flip your current leader face down and set it aside, choose a new leader from those face up, and place on your faction board. Tuck your Loyal Viziers into the Decree spaces listed on your new leader. If you must choose a new leader but none are face up, flip them all face up.
  4. Rest: End Daylight and begin Evening.

🦡 Woodland Alliance (The Guerrilla Insurgency)

Stoke the flames of rebellion from the shadows, spread sympathy, and overthrow ruling empires.

Faction Rules & Abilities

  • Guerrilla War: As the defender in battle, you deal hits equal to the higher roll, and the attacker deals hits equal to the lower roll.
  • The Supporters Stack Rule: Supporters are kept face down and don't count against your main hand size limit. If you have NO bases on the board, your stack is strictly capped at a maximum of 5 cards! Additional incoming cards are discarded.
  • Outrage Tracking: Whenever an opponent moves units into your sympathetic clearing or destroys a token, they must hand a matching card from their hand directly into your Supporters Stack.

Faction Setup

  1. Gather Warriors: Form a supply of 10 warriors.
  2. Place Bases: Place 3 bases on the respective spaces in your bases box.
  3. Fill Sympathy Track: Place 10 sympathy tokens on your Sympathy track.
  4. Gain Supporters: Draw 3 cards and place them face down on your Supporters stack.

Removing Bases Penalty

Martyrdom Costs: If an established faction base is wiped off the map, you must instantly:

  • Discard all supporters matching that clearing's suit (including all Bird wildcards).
  • Remove half of your trained Officers pool (rounded up).
  • If no bases remain, immediately discard down to the 5-card maximum storage cap!

Turn Phases & Action Economy

Birdsong

Revolt (Any number of times): If you have no cards in your hand, draw one card.

  1. Choose Clearing: Choose a sympathetic clearing without a base that matches a base on your faction board
  2. Spend Supporters: Spend 2 supporters matching the suit of the chosen clearing
  3. Resolve Effects: Remove all enemy pieces from the chosen clearing. Place the matching base there, and place warriors there equal to the number of sympathetic clearings matching the base’s printed suit. Place one warrior in the Officers box.

Spread Sympathy (Any number of times):

  1. Choose Clearing: Choose an unsympathetic clearing adjacent to a sympathetic clearing. If there are no sympathetic clearings, you may choose any clearing.
  2. Spend Supporters: Spend supporters matching the suit of the chosen clearing. The number of supporters that must be spent is listed above the sympathy token. You must spend another matching supporter if the target clearing has at least 3 warriors of another player, including warriors they are treating as their own for rule.
  3. Place and Score: Place a sympathy token in the chosen clearing. Score the VP on the space uncovered on your faction board.

🦝 The Vagabond (The Lone Wanderer)

Navigate the Woodland conflicts as a solitary rogue, trading items, running quests, or forming secret coalitions.

Faction Rules & Abilities

  • Lone Wanderer: Your pawn is not a warrior. You cannot rule clearings, you cannot block others from ruling, and you can never be removed from the map.
  • Nimble & Slip: You can move freely regardless of who rules the origin or destination clearing. During Birdsong, your Slip let's you move 1 adjacent space or deep into a forest completely for free.
  • Defenseless: If you are targeted in a battle and have zero undamaged Swords face up or face down in your inventory, you are defenseless and take an extra hit.
  • Coalitions: You cannot win via standard Dominance card rule checks. Instead, in 4+ player games, you can activate a Dominance card to tether your score marker to the player with the lowest VPs. You discard your point pool, and if they win, you share the victory!
  • Crafting: Exhaust a hammer to activate its pawn as a crafting piece, ignoring the once-per-turn limit.
  • Maximum Rolled Hits: In battle, the Vagabond’s max rolled hits equals to his undamaged sword, face up or face down, in his Satchel.
  • Taking Hits: For every hit you take in battle, you must select one undamaged item and move it to the Damaged Box.
  • Capacity Constraints: In Evening, you must permanently destroy items that exceed your satchel limit (calculated as 6 items base +2 items per face-up Satchel on your track).
  • Forest Movement: Your Slip allows you to move in and out of forests. Other Move actions only let you move out of a forest into an adjacent clearing, not into a forest.
  • Relationships: Your faction board shows a Relationships chart, which has 4 spaces on the Allied track and 1 Hostile box.
    1. Improving Relationships: Improve a relationship with a non-Hostile faction by taking the Aid action. Aid a non-Hostile faction the number of times listed between their current Allied space and their next Allied space during the same turn.

      2. A given Aid action counts toward only one improvement in relationship. Advance that faction’s relationship marker one space right on the track. Score the VPs listed in the new space.

    2. Allied Status: If a relationship marker reaches the final space on the Allied track, you’re now allied with the faction shown by that relationship marker. Each time you Aid an Allied faction, score 2 VPs. When you move, you may force warriors of one Allied faction to move along with your Vagabond pawn to your destination clearing. When you move alone, such as with the Harrier’s ability, you can’t move Allied warriors.

      3. At the start of battle as the attacker, you may treat the warriors of one Allied faction in the clearing of battle as your own. The max hits you can roll equals the number of their warriors there + your total undamaged swords. You can’t treat Allied warriors as your own when attacking the same Allied faction.

      In a battle where you treat Allied warriors as your own, you can take hits by removing them. However, if you take more hits by removing Allied warriors than by damaging items during the same battle, that Allied faction becomes Hostile at the end of the battle. This rule overrides the condition for becoming Hostile.

    3. Hostile Status: If you remove a warrior of a non-Hostile faction, move their relationship marker to the Hostile box. Then, if this happened in battle during your turn, check for Infamy scoring (+1 VP for each piece of a Hostile faction you remove in battle during your turn, except the warrior that made the faction Hostile).

      4. You must exhaust an extra boot to move into a clearing with any warriors of any Hostile faction. You can’t move a relationship marker out of the Hostile box by using Aid, but you can still Aid a Hostile faction to take crafted items. You can form a coalition with a Hostile faction. If you do, move their relationship marker to the Indifferent space.

Faction Setup

  1. Choose Character: Choose a character card and place it in your Character Card slot.
  2. Place Pawn: Place your Vagabond pawn in any forest.
  3. Get Quests: Shuffle your quest deck, draw 3 quest cards, and place them face up near you.
  4. Populate Ruins: Take the 4 ruins from the map, and take the satchel, boot, hammer, and sword items marked with an “R”. Place 1 beneath each ruin and then shuffle each ruin stack. Then, return each stack to an empty ruin slot on the map.
  5. Take Starting Items: Take the items marked with an “S” listed on your character card. Place any listed kettle, stack of coins, and satchel items face up on the respective tracks of your faction board. Place all other listed items face up in your Satchel. Return any remaining “S” items to the box.
  6. Set Relationships: Take a relationship marker for each non-Vagabond faction and place them all on the Indifferent space of your Relationships chart.

Turn Phases & Action Economy

Birdsong

Refresh: Flip 2 exhausted items face up for each kettle face up on the Refresh track, not counting the kettle that you flip face up in this step. Then flip up 3 more exhausted items.

Slip: You may move into an adjacent clearing or forest without exhausting any boots, even for moving into a Hostile clearing. This move ignores all effects that prevent movement out of a clearing.

Daylight

Take these actions in any order and number:

🐹 Underground Duchy (The Subterranean Empire)

Deploy underground tunnels from your private subterranean Burrow, sway political ministers, and manage a fragile empire.

Faction Rules & Abilities

  • The Burrow & Tunnels: The Burrow is a private, unsuited clearing adjacent to every clearing with a Tunnel token. Non-Duchy pieces can never be placed in or moved into the Burrow, and you always rule it automatically. You have a hard cap of 3 Tunnel tokens on the map.
  • Crafting: The Duchy crafts during Evening by activating citadels and markets.
  • The Price of Failure: Whenever any number of Duchy buildings are removed, return your swayed minister card of the highest rank (Lord, then Noble, then Squire) back to the Unswayed pile. Permanently remove that minister's crown from the game entirely (reducing your scoring potential). Discard 1 random card from your hand.

Faction Setup

  1. Gather Warriors & Tunnels: Form supplies of 20 warriors and 3 tunnel tokens.
  2. Prepare the Burrow: Place the Burrow near the map.
  3. Surface: Place 2 warriors and 1 tunnel in a corner clearing that is not the starting corner clearing of another player and, if possible, is diagonally opposite from a starting corner clearing. Then place 2 warriors in each clearing adjacent to the chosen corner clearing, except the Burrow.
  4. Fill Buildings Tracks: Place 3 citadels and 3 markets on your respective Buildings spaces.
  5. Collect Ministers: Place 9 minister cards face up on your Unswayed Ministers pile.
  6. Fill Crown Spaces: Place 9 crowns on the spaces with VPs on your faction board.

🐦 Corvid Conspiracy (The Hidden Insurgency)

Distract your opponents, plant hidden traps, and exploit the Woodland through a network of secret plots.

Faction Rules & Abilities

  • Nimble: You can move freely regardless of who rules the origin or destination clearing. Clearings are strictly limited to 1 plot token max.
  • Embedded Agents: As a defender in battle, if you have a facedown plot token in the clearing, you automatically deal +1 extra hit to the attacker (even if you are defenseless).
  • Exposure Risk: Enemies can show you a matching card to guess a facedown plot's identity. If they are correct, the token is disarmed and removed. If they are wrong, they must hand that card directly into your hand!
  • Crafting: Craft during Birdsong by activating plot tokens (whether face up or down).
  • Plot Tokens: 8 plot tokens, 2 of each type. While in the Corvids’ supply, plot tokens are face down. On the map, a plot token is can be face up or face down. Corvids can inspect any facedown plot token at any time. Each clearing can hold only 1 plot token.

Faction Setup

  1. Gather Warriors & Tunnels: Form supplies of 15 warriors and 8 plot tokens face down.
  2. Scatter: Place 1 warrior in any clearing of each suit (total 3).

Turn Phases & Action Economy

Birdsong

Craft: You may activate plot tokens to craft cards from your hand.

Flip Plots: Any number of times, flip a plot token face up in a clearing with any Corvid warriors, score 1 VP per face-up plot token on the map (including the newly flipped one), and then resolve its flip effect if it is a bomb or extortion.

Recruit: Once per turn, you may spend any card to place one warrior in each matching clearing. If you spend a bird, choose one suit to place warriors in.

Daylight

Take up to 3 actions, as follows, in any order and number.

Action Mechanic Execution Rules
Move Take a move.
Plot Remove a Corvid warrior + another Corvid warrior per plot you have placed this turn, from a clearing with no plot tokens to place a face-down plot token there.
Battle Initiate a battle.
Trick Swap 2 plot tokens on the map. Both plot tokens must be face-up or face-down.
Evening

Exert: You may take 1 action listed in Daylight if you choose not to draw cards during Evening.

Draw: Draw 1 card + 1 card per face-up extortion token on the map. If your hand exceeds 5 cards, discard down to 5.

The Underground Conspiracy Arsenal

Plot Token Flip Effect & Strategy Profile
💣 Bomb When flipped, detonates to remove all enemy pieces in its clearing. Devastating for breaking up massive enemy garrisons.
📣 Raid Acts as a dynamic spawn point. If destroyed, it triggers in your favor. (If removed via standard Exposure, it does not place extra warriors).
💰 Extortion When flipped, instantly steals a card from a player in that clearing. In Evening, grants +1 extra card draw per active face-up Extortion on the map.
🕸️ Snare Locks down paths. Prevents other players from moving pieces out of this clearing entirely.

Strategic Strengths

  • If people guess incorrectly, they get free cards.
  • When a raid is destroyed, it works in their favour.
  • Able to place 3 warriors at a time.
  • The Embedded Agents ability is game-changing.

Critical Weaknesses

  • Hard to earn VPs.
  • If their tokens are targeted, they can’t do anything.
  • Not able to group up warriors effectively.
  • Weak cavalry.
